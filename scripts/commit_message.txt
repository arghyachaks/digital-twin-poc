ML steps 3-5: trained pump condition models, SHAP explanations, LSTM challenger

- ml/train.py: Isolation Forest anomaly model, XGBoost fault classifier and
  quantile remaining-life models (p10/p50/p90), evaluated on 60 unseen pumps
  and logged to MLflow (sqlite). Production bundle v1 committed in ml/models.
- v1 results: warning 195 h before failure vs 88 h for the ISO alarm,
  0.01% false alarms, macro-F1 0.993, RUL MAE 38 h (25 h in the last 100 h).
- ml/predict.py: live scoring with TreeSHAP reasons (why this fault, what
  shortens remaining life); twin shows WATCH status, ML card and AI events;
  assistant gets a get_ml_assessment tool.
- ml/train_lstm.py: PyTorch LSTM RUL challenger on 24 h raw sequences,
  compared head-to-head with the boosted model, logged to MLflow.
- Windows Smart App Control: pin scipy 1.15.3, scikit-learn 1.6.1 and
  sqlalchemy<2.1; MLflow failures no longer stop a model from being saved.
- UI files served with no-cache so updates show without a hard refresh.
